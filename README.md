# The Caretaker
Demo:https://lwhsbg.itch.io/the-caretaker

## What It Is
A short mystery horror game made in RenPy. You play as a caretaker in a mysterious mansion where the owner does not reveal himself. You unravel the truth  of that place through on as the days go by. 

## Why I Made It

I wanted to try making a horror mystery where you have your different choices tackle the outcome of the event. I was inspired by the mysterious mansion themes and wanted to make a horror game relating to that.

## How I Made It

- Ren'Py

## What I Learnt And What I Struggled With

What I learnt was definitely dealing with the sizing of my sprites better. I also learnt how to deal with the suspicion system which was fun to think of and I also learnt about how the backgrounds can be dealt with easily using transform which i had struggled with in the last projects.
